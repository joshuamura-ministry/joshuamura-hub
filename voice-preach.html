Paste your manuscript with the @@@ marks where slides change, then read aloud. As you reach each mark it advances the sermon slide — on the projector, the booth, and everyone following live. Your clicker always works too.

This browser doesn't offer speech recognition. On iPad, open this page in Safari. On a laptop, use Chrome, Edge, or Safari.
If your manuscript had @@@ marks, the slide turns below are already placed for you — each one sits on the last word before the @@@, so the slide flips right as you begin the new section. To adjust: tap a marker to remove it, or tap any word to add one there. Markers renumber themselves (you begin on slide 1).
Sermon slides: upload this sermon's JPEGs once into their own folder in your hub (e.g. /SERMONS/john-mark/ with 1.jpg, 2.jpg…), then name that folder here. Nothing gets overwritten — each sermon keeps its own set. Leave the folder blank to use the plain /SERMONS/ slides.
